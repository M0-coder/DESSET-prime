About DESSET

DESSET brings digital commerce, technology and logistics together under a common identity. Our purpose is to increase the value of time for people and businesses.

Business areas

DESSET

Digital commerce, ordering and last-mile delivery.

DESSET prime

Parcel logistics, transportation and distribution. Our logistics vision is intended to evolve in stages; it does not imply active international operations.

Our principles

  • Integrity and transparency.
  • Reliability and responsible execution.
  • Efficiency and respect for resources.
  • Useful innovation and continuous learning.
